## Independent review and remaining acceptance gates

An independent read-only visual/security reviewer inspected the actual source, Storybook/Windows captures and code-level QA. Initial findings included **RTL utility-side overlap**, width-transition layout thrash, hardcoded shadow outside design tokens, a native GDI desktop recorder that could capture unrelated windows, premature entrance screenshots and stale evidence risks. The working Draft **removed** the unsafe desktop recorder (and all its videos), replaced it with target-only CDP native renderer frames, mirrored the RTL connected path and physically aligned utility strip, added an RTL geometry browser regression, eliminated the outer rail-zone width tween, used the shared shadow token, and now waits for entrance/spring settling for still screenshots. **These corrections require independent re-review on the final committed Draft SHA**; do not treat the initial review as passed signoff.

The owner's new direct V5-style example prompted a complete scene/CSS visual rework after the initial reviewer findings: the dense dark hero, fake metric strip, stacked KPI/chart cards and excessive visual badges are gone. Real new Chromium screenshots of RTL and 320×256/200% exposed **two additional actual defects**: an English question mark reordered under RTL and invisible dark-on-dark text when transparency was disabled. Both were corrected with language-aware bidi rendering and theme-aware opaque-mode ink. The new `ReferencePrototype` preserves all twelve Storybook variant IDs and functional route, sample-plan, reading/reflection/reset controls. Seven focused Playwright cases cover empty/synthetic honesty, mobile navigation and overflow, 200% reading continuation, interruptible connected morph, RTL physical spine geometry, motion-off/high-contrast and tagged WCAG axe screening **for all 12 Storybook variants plus 390px mobile**, with no horizontal overflow at tested dimensions. This variant screen waits until the route entrance has settled (`.rp-scene` computed opacity greater than 0.999); an initial *premature* contrast scan saw the fade's partly transparent paper as the dark backdrop, while the fully opaque reading surface renders dark ink over a solid light matte panel. Do not interpret a settled-screen axe pass as measured transient-frame contrast. Axe still reports **incomplete `color-contrast` and `aria-prohibited-attr` cases** on some variations; actual contrast sampling, animated transitions and the original human AT/20-case matrix remain **NOT CERTIFIED/NOT RUN**. The independent review also corrected a real readability risk by placing the Reading contents on an opaque matte surface rather than directly over the photo. Focused tests and emulated axe scans do **not** replace native human AT testing.

**Supply-chain/secret-check fidelity:** An earlier feature-HEAD hosted Secret scan identified the QA-only executable's 64-character SHA-256 stored in tracked screencast metadata as a high-entropy string. This was not a credential, but rather than weaken the fail-closed scanner or add an exception, the final native capture writes this provenance **only into ignored local** `.git/m0-005-visual-native/last-captured-executable-sha256.txt`; committed JSON retains source frame counts, distinct counts, observed span and scope disclaimers, but not that hex literal. The real WebView2 screencast was regenerated after the change. Local unchanged `detect-secrets==1.5.0` reported zero *new* suspicious strings; the revised exact-head hosted Secret scan is still required, and the previous red run remains in GitHub history.
