# Prespecified formative analysis, blank data dictionary and finding trace v0.1

**No observations, participants, survey responses, recordings, findings or results exist in this package.** This is a human-use plan *before* recruitment, not an analytic report or significance test. Canonical stages and forms: [01 protocol](01-protocol.md); math answer/scoring anchors: [04 key](04-answer-key.md); observable codes: [06 sheet](06-observation-sheet.md); interview: [07 guide](07-interview-guide.md). Data entry is **human-only** in approved secure storage; the [CSV templates](templates) contain **headers only**.

## 1. Data security and identity separation

A human assigns P001–P010 only **after approved consent**. Names/contact/consent signatures, model provider permission and pseudonym mapping are retained **separately** from research tables in a private location approved by the responsible investigator, not in Git or an unapproved cloud. **Pseudonymization is not anonymization**, especially for 5–10 people: individual profiles, raw observations and evidence references are accessible **only to authorized private reviewers**. Public output is non-identifying aggregated counts with small/distinctive cells, identifiable examples, combinations and quotes **suppressed or omitted** where disclosure risk remains; no public pseudonymized case profiles. Raw answers, quoted personal speech and recordings are **not required** for initial structured analysis. If the organizer approves recordings and separate participants opt in, enforce disclosed storage/deletion deadlines; no recording by default. Distinguish locally enforceable deletion and backups from provider deletion **requests** and copies beyond organizer control; never promise full provider erasure. Model/provider fields, terms, region, retention, access, institutional decisions and request channel remain **unfilled human execution gates** in [02](02-information-consent.md).

## 3. Scoring and prespecified denominators

- **Independent baseline (0–4):** sum of the *two* offered, validly unassisted, human-scorable B1+B2 for A or E1+E2 for B. **Independent exit (0–4):** E1+E2 for A or B1+B2 for B. **Transfer (0–4):** T1+T2 independently. A correct value with no derivative work is at most 1 per [key](04-answer-key.md); T1 units and interpretation use the key's anchors.
- **Presented and skipped versus missing/contaminated:** a participant who actually sees an independent item and explicitly skips earns **0** with `attempt_status=skipped`. **Not offered** is its planned row with `presented=NO,not_observed`, score blank. **Presented but unverifiable** has `missing_evidence`, score blank. **Help, hint, model intrusion or prior held-out answer leakage** makes it `contaminated`, assistance recorded, score **blank for independent analysis even if the answer happens to be correct**. Withdrawn consent follows erasure instructions and may remove private rows entirely. A block with any unscorable item has **no complete two-item total**; preserve actual available evidence but do not impute a value or count an incomplete pair.
- **Paired descriptive change:** only participants with all four independently valid, human-scored baseline and exit items have delta = exit − baseline (range −4…4). Restrict **individual pseudonymized paired profiles to authorized private reviewers**; publish only non-identifying aggregate n and median/range where privacy-safe, otherwise suppress small cells and arm breakdowns. Not a controlled effect or delayed-retention test; A/B forms have uncalibrated difficulty.
- **Transfer:** report T1 and T2 correct/partial/incorrect and full 0–4 separately from baseline/exit. Immediate transfer after a short tutorial is **not delayed retrieval**. Follow-up 2–5 days, if ever desired, requires a separately approved human protocol; no retention entries are inferred now.
- **Confidence/calibration:** optional 1–5 before feedback, report only descriptive counts by correctness/confidence among answered opportunities. No Brier scores, psychometric accuracy or longitudinal calibration from one short session.
- **Model behavior per opportunity:** for each code use denominator **YES+NO** among observed, eligible instances. Report **YES/N** or **NO/N as directionally appropriate** plus separate counts of NOT_OBSERVED, NOT_APPLICABLE and MISSING_EVIDENCE, not percentages across all recruits. For violations (PREMATURE_FULL_SOLUTION, EXCESSIVE_HINT, SYCOPHANTIC_AGREEMENT), YES means harm/violation; for desired acts (DIAGNOSE_FIRST, HINT_APPROPRIATE, HONEST_UNCERTAINTY, SOURCE_ADHERENCE, LEARNER_CONTROL), YES means observed compliance. **Never report a rate without its code definition and eligible denominator.**
- **Moderator interventions:** numerator = distinct `intervention_id` on **guided-stage** MODERATOR_INTERVENTION rows (one per real corrective action); denominator = distinct `model_turn_id` on verified **guided-stage** MODEL_TURN rows for visible responses (including partials). Do not derive either count from repeated behavior rows, count non-guided human assistance, or reuse an intervention ID. Whole-session human actions (consent, baseline, interview, interruption) are separately described as minimal session/incident events, **not** this numerator. Reconcile numerator to `sessions.moderator_interventions_total`, with observed reason codes. Only report `interventions / observed guided turns` when `turn_log_status=COMPLETE` and turn count positive; otherwise rate **undefined/insufficient turn evidence** (including NO_TURNS or INCOMPLETE), show separate distinct counts and TECHNICAL_FAILURE/MISSING_EVIDENCE cases. For a pooled descriptive actions-per-turn rate, sum numerators and denominators **only across COMPLETE positive-turn sessions**, and disclose the count of excluded/incomplete sessions; never silently pool unknown turn logs. This is actions-per-turn, **not a probability** and may exceed 1. A correction cannot retroactively make model behavior PASS.
- **Interview:** 1–5 only for answered clarity/control anchored items; report observed distributions/median/range and number missing, plus themes with actual participant counts. A skipped question is missing rather than neutral 3.

## 4. Prespecified design discussion flags — not statistical efficacy thresholds

Review immediately if *any* model gives a premature full guided answer, affirms a mathematically false claim without checking, invents a packet citation, violates materials-only source, overrules checked math or ignores a stop; stop the session promptly for any actual unconsented data transfer or serious supported wellbeing/safety concern. Human determines whether another session is appropriate after an observed defect; no claim these checks validate model safety.

For formative discussion **only**, inspect the proportion of participants with both complete exit items reaching at least 3/4 points, the proportion with both complete transfer tasks reaching at least 2/4, and median voluntary clarity/control ratings (a median of 4/5 is a possible design-review target). Also inspect whether **at least half** of the actually scorable participants meet each task target; if not, note a design question. Report all actual numerators, denominators and missingness; **these are arbitrary small-sample design prompts, not pass/fail gates, causal effects, statistical significance or validated scales**. A participant declining questions is not a study “failure.”

## 5. Qualitative coding without artificial precision

A human reviewer labels each non-identifying actual episode with an optional **fact code** (WHAT HAPPENED) and separately an **interpretive theme** (WHY IT MIGHT MATTER). Prespecified themes: CLARITY_OK, CLARITY_CONFUSING, PACE_FAST, PACE_SLOW, HINT_USEFUL, HINT_EXCESS, CONTROL_SUPPORTED, CONTROL_LOST, SOURCE_CLEAR, SOURCE_UNCLEAR, TRUST_CORRECTION, TRUST_MISSTATEMENT, FRUSTRATION_OBSERVED, REPAIR_HELPFUL, NEXT_CHANGE_REQUESTED. Also allow **UNCLASSIFIED**, **CONTRADICTORY**, **NOT_OBSERVED**, **MISSING_EVIDENCE**, and explicitly labeled new themes.

Do not infer psychological traits, diagnoses, motivation or verified mastery from tone. Two reviewers may independently code a subset of minimal approved notes and discuss disagreements; if only one trained reviewer is available, say so. Negative/disconfirming cases are reported, not excluded. Quote direct participant speech only when consent and minimization permit, remove direct identifiers, store locally with controlled access; small-n quotes can re-identify people, so consider paraphrase instead.

## 7. Findings → specification changes and known limitations

After real sessions, a *human* writes a **public aggregate-only, disclosure-reviewed** findings note, with any individual evidence references retained in the authorized **private** trace, uses the blank [change trace CSV](templates/change-trace.csv) to propose **specific** possible updates to [docs/06 tutor behavior](../../06-tutor-behavior-spec.md) or [docs/11 session orchestration](../../11-session-orchestration-and-flows.md), and seeks independent review/owner approval. Do **not** edit the constitution or flow because an agent predicts a finding. Some findings may support **no change**; document that explicitly.

**Validity limits:** a convenience sample of only 5–10 adults, one mathematics packet, potential selection bias, non-equated alternating forms and test familiarity, tutor/model/version variability, moderator corrections, interview demand effects, missing/withdrawn responses and no untreated control group. There are no delayed tasks, grades, real model calls or actual causal outcomes. Do not calculate inferential p-values, effect sizes, causal “gain,” subject-general conclusions, 30/90-day retention, clinical/educational treatment benefit or statistical generalizability.
