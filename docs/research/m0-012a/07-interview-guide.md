# Neutral short interview guide v0.1 — optional, 36–43 minutes

**Human-only protocol instrument; no interview has occurred.** Ask only after the independent exit/transfer block, never during it. Participation and answering each question are voluntary. Use [02-information-consent](02-information-consent.md) and [09-human-runbook](09-human-runbook.md); no diagnoses, personal grades, disability history, email, live-course assignment, identity or protected data are requested. Record a minimal non-identifying theme/optional rating in approved secure storage, not Git.

## Opening (moderator script)

“Thanks for trying the synthetic questions. I'd like to know what was clear or confusing, not grade you or get a preferred answer. You can skip any question, correct me, pause or stop. Your honest criticism is just as useful as a positive view. This is a prototype, not a demonstrated teaching tool.”

## Core neutral questions (ask as time permits; do not bias or rescue answers)

1. **Clarity:** “Which part of the tutor's explanation, if any, was easiest to follow? Which part was hard to interpret?” Optional: “Could you point to a moment?”
2. **Pacing:** “How did the speed and length of explanations feel?” Optional: “When would a different pace have helped?”
3. **Helpfulness:** “At what moment, if any, did the conversation help your reasoning? Was there anything that did not help?” Optional: “What would you change?”
4. **Hints versus direct answers:** “How did you decide when to ask for a hint or a more direct explanation?” Optional: “What would you have preferred at that point?”
5. **Agency/control:** “Were there times you wanted to pause, skip, change direction or stop? What happened?” Optional: “Was it easy to do so?”
6. **Correctness and honesty:** “Did anything the tutor said seem wrong, overconfident, or too agreeable with an answer? Or did it seem appropriately cautious?” Optional: “What made you think that?”
7. **Sources:** “Could you tell whether an explanation came from the synthetic packet or from somewhere else?” Optional: “When would you want a stronger source label?”
8. **Frustration and wellbeing:** “Were there moments of confusion or frustration, if any, and how did the tutor respond?” Optional: “You can pass on this.”
9. **Confidence in understanding:** “What part of the topic would you now feel willing to try independently? What are you still unsure about?” This is **self-report, not verified mastery**.
10. **Improvements:** “What is the one change you would make before another learner tries this format?” Optional: “Anything important we didn't ask?”

Do **not** ask “Did the excellent tutor help you learn?” or “Surely the hints were useful?”; avoid promises of learning gain, permanent retention or actual exam performance. For negative feedback, ask an equally neutral specific follow-up; do not defend the prototype.

## Optional anchored single-item impressions (not validated psychometric scales)

- “How **clear** were the explanations for your own tasks?” **1 = very unclear; 2 = somewhat unclear; 3 = mixed; 4 = mostly clear; 5 = very clear.**
- “How much **control** did you feel over hints, pace and whether to stop?” **1 = no control; 2 = little; 3 = mixed; 4 = quite a lot; 5 = full control.**
- Optional question, not a scale: “Would you prefer more hints, more direct answers, or depends on the problem? Why?”
- Always offer **prefer not to answer**, record missing; no researcher-imputed value and no psychometric validity claim.

## Moderator close

“Would you like to add anything, change an earlier answer or withdraw your notes under the explained procedure? [Human organizer confirms the real approved withdrawal contact and retention policy.] This short activity cannot tell us whether you would remember the topic days later or perform better on an exam. Thank you for any feedback you chose to share.”

**Coding:** record question ID (Q1–Q10, R-CLARITY, R-CONTROL), answered/skipped/missing, optional anchored rating, minimal pseudonymized theme codes from [08](08-analysis-plan.md) and evidence reference if approved. Do not record raw interviews, audio or screens unless separately authorized and consented.
