## Gate 2 · Session stages (candidate 43 minutes, max 45 only if willing)

1. **00–04 — HUMAN ONLY goal and feasibility.** Ask what a short chain/product practice would usefully cover; explain one session cannot promise mastery, exam readiness or durable retention. State source mode **materials_only**, packet synthetic, no actual course document. Record goal category only if necessary, never grade/identity. **Do not open the model conversation or paste D1.**
2. **04–10 — independent baseline.** Arm **A** gets **B1/B2**, arm **B** gets **E1/E2**. No model, hints or correctness feedback. Capture each offered/skipped attempt, its method and *optional* confidence **1–5 before any feedback**. Do not infer confidence. Defer keys to private moderator.
3. **10–15 — HUMAN ONLY retrieval/worked examples.** Ask **R1** before showing **W1/W2** with actual source labels [packet §3/§4]. Let learner explain chain inner multiplier and both product terms. Teaching process only, **do not send D3 to a model**.
4. **15–27 — ONLY permitted model-guided phase.** At minute 15 start a freshly approved conversation with [05 initial and synthetic §3 source prompts](05-prototype-prompts.md), after baseline/warm-up. Arm A G1→G2, arm B G2→G1, then **I1 first unaided**. I2, fictional claim **C1** and source gap **S1** are optional; send C1/S1 *uncued* question only without moderator key or anticipated answer. Paste one current guided item at a time and approved minimum text only; **no confidence/errors/verdicts sent by default**. Human privately verifies, gives checked feedback and mandatory brief calibration when a recorded confidence mismatch occurs. For ordinary hints use 0→1→2→3→4→5; explicitly chosen/recorded level-4 fast jump is possible after a reflection. Level 5 requires **completed level 4 OR explicit learner request after short reflection**, plus human permission and **new, never-seen checked F1/F2 similar retry**, never held-out B/E/T. Log fast-path/assisted status and actual model responses. Record violations before correction.
5. **27–36 — HUMAN ONLY independent exit + transfer.** **At minute 27 terminate model interaction and close/hide the guided chat.** Arm A receives E1/E2, B receives B1/B2 (prioritize both through about minute 32), then T1/T2 through minute 36. No hints/answers/feedback; confidence optional before feedback. If the participant saw a held-out solution early or got a hint/model input during an independent attempt, flag `contaminated` and assistance with **blank independent score**, excluded from pairs; do not invent clean scoring. Allow skip or stop.
6. **36–43 — HUMAN ONLY neutral optional interview/debrief.** Ask [07 questions](07-interview-guide.md) without assuming benefit; collect optional clarity/control ratings and minimal themes. The model does not produce D10 summary or interview. Explicitly say this cannot demonstrate long-term retention or graded outcomes. Reiterate actual human withdrawal/deletion route.
7. **Hard stage/cutoff policy:** stop warm-up at 15, prioritize both G items and I1 by 23; if time tight omit optional activities **I2 then S1 then C1**, never extend guided model beyond 27. At 36 stop assessments, mark unoffered B/E/T as `presented=NO,not_observed`, not numeric zero. At 43 finish; allow up to 45 only on voluntary affirmative request. Never rush or trim outcomes to hide errors. An actually **offered and skipped** task is 0; missing evidence and contaminated attempts have no independent score. Time overrun/partial completion is recorded, not repaired or disguised.

## Gate 3 · Interruption, distress, model error or consent withdrawal

- **Participant says stop or withdraw:** halt immediately, including model input/output and optional recording; follow approved local identity/link/record/backup erasure steps, request provider-side deletion only as supported by actual terms, and explain provider-retained limits rather than claiming guaranteed deletion. No academic or other penalty.
- **Fatigue/frustration:** acknowledge, offer short break/easier path or end; do not diagnose, provoke, coerce, infer health information or continue as if consent persists. Serious wellbeing concerns are handled by approved human safeguarding/support roles, not a tutoring model.
- **Unconsented content exposure or provider change:** stop all transfers, follow human incident/reporting/deletion plan; do not switch destinations to salvage the session. Future permission cannot retroactively authorize past data handling.
- **Wrong mathematical claim, unjustified agreement, premature active solution, fake packet citation or outside-material teaching:** log *actual* observed model behavior first; verify against separate key, correct visibly if appropriate, let human determine safe continuation. Never hide the failure by prompting the model again until “good.”
- **Invalid/unclear output or provider outage:** mark **MISSING_EVIDENCE** / technical failure rather than invent a response; use static synthetic explanation only if human-approved and label intervention. Human decides whether to abort the model portion.
- **Accessibility barrier:** offer an accessible, unpressured alternative or stop; record high-level barrier without a diagnosis or speculative cause. No human case in the canonical product's separate accessibility matrix may be marked PASS from this study.
