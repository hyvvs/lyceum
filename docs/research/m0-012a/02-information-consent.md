## Information for a prospective adult participant

**Study title:** Exploring a short, moderated mathematics tutor conversation (Lyceum M0-012A prototype). **Organizing investigator/contact:** `[REQUIRED: responsible investigator name, institution if any, verified contact and withdrawal contact]`. **Independent privacy/research contact:** `[REQUIRED: responsible privacy/ethics contact]`. **Applicable institutional/research-ethics decision/reference:** `[REQUIRED: actual approval/exemption/not-required determination by competent human authority]`.

**Why:** We are exploring whether a prototype tutoring conversation is understandable, honest and gives students useful control over hints. This is **not** a test of your intelligence, an official class assignment, treatment, a medical/psychological trial or a proven tutoring tool.

**Who:** Adults aged **18 or older** who have encountered the basic power rule and elementary calculus. Participation is optional and not tied to an instructor’s grades, enrollment, funding, academic standing, access to teaching or evaluation. You do not need to share your actual grades, diagnoses, disability history, personal experiences of distress or detailed academic records.

**What happens:** One private **about 43-minute** session (approximately **40–45 minutes**) with a human moderator. You may choose a small goal, attempt a few original polynomial-derivative questions before seeing examples, explore hints with a supervised prototype chat model and work on new questions independently, then optionally share what was clear or confusing. You can decline any question, ask for an accessible format, take a break or end the session at any time. No bonus/penalty for right or wrong answers. Some responses may be incorrect or misleading because the model is experimental; the moderator checks mathematical answers separately.

**What is collected if you agree:** a randomly assigned pseudonym (not your name), which task/stage you saw, optional 1–5 answer confidence and interview ratings, answer correctness rubric (0–2) and general error category, hint level, whether the tutor asked about reasoning, premature answers or source labels, moderator interventions, stage durations, and optional brief non-identifying feedback. These **pseudonymized records are not guaranteed anonymous**: especially with only 5–10 people, a profile or quote could identify someone in context. Direct names, exact course grades, account identifiers, diagnoses or personal histories are **not** needed or requested. A separate human-controlled consent/contact record and any identity-to-pseudonym link must be kept **separate from study evidence** for withdrawal processing, with tightly limited authorized access. Avoid typing personal details into prototype chats.

The approved moderator/authorized analyst may access only necessary private notes.
